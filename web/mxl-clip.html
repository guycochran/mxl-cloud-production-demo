MXL Clipper

The first non-linear editor for time-addressable media

This is a live show being produced entirely in the cloud — switched, keyed and mixed in EBU MXL shared memory, then written to a BBC TAMS store as it happens. You're editing it while it records.

1
Scrub by time, not by file. Drag the filmstrip. Every frame is addressed by its capture timecode, pulled live from the TAMS store.
2
Mark in & out. Press I and O. Cue off the PGM audio waveform — a laugh, applause, a beat. Drop M markers to flag moments.
3
Grab the clip. It's cut in your browser — no server render, no upload — and downloads a frame-accurate MP4 in about a second.
