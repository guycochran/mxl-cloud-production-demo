Why "smallest complete" is the goal

You could add endlessly to any one layer — more switcher features, fancier graphics, more codecs. That's the vMix road, and it's not ours. The more useful thing is to prove the whole vertical works end-to-end with open standards at every layer, as small as it can be while still being complete. That's what turns a demo into a laboratory: each box is a place the community can swap in their own implementation and see if the seams hold.

Three of these layers are frontier work — control (IN-001 is a draft), Fabrics (the API is still evolving), and AI (just becoming a funded research question). That's exactly where field data from a running facility is worth most. Start at what MXL is, or read what broke when we ran the whole stack live.
