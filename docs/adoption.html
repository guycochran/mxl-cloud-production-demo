Who's building on MXL

MXL went from an EBU idea to a Linux Foundation project with a roster of broadcasters and vendors behind it — and by IBC 2026 real products shipped native support. This is a source-cited snapshot: every entry links to where the claim comes from, and anything we couldn't verify from a primary source is flagged as such.

Founding roster (Linux Foundation, April 2025)

When MXL was announced as a Linux Foundation project, the launch named an initial set of broadcaster end-users and solution providers. Per the announcement:

Broadcaster end-usersSolution providers
BBC, CBC/Radio-Canada, Bell Media, Dome Productions, France Télévisions, OBS (Olympic Broadcasting Services), RTÉ, SRG SSR, SVT, SWR/ARD, VRTAppear, AWS, Grass Valley, Intel, Lawo, NVIDIA, Riedel, Telos Alliance

That's an unusually broad coalition for a day-one open-source media project — public broadcasters and commercial vendors committing together is the signal that MXL isn't one company's bet.

Shipped at IBC 2026 — native MXL in products

A year on, the move from "members" to "products" is the real milestone. These are vendors who demonstrated native MXL support, each with a primary or dated trade-press source:

Grass Valley — native MXL v1.1 in AMPP + a hardware gateway

Native MXL v1.1 support in the AMPP platform, NMOS flow discovery/control, and the ACE-3901-GRID gateway bridging SDI / ST 2110 to MXL — an end-to-end MXL-integrated workflow. Source: Grass Valley · TVBEurope.

Evertz — native MXL across Reflektor, DreamCatcher & cVIP

Reflektor as an on-ramp converting SDI / ST 2110 / SRT / Zixi / RIST into MXL; DreamCatcher for native MXL ingest, replay and playout; the cVIP multiviewer reading MXL. Also shown at the AWS and Oracle booths. Source: StorageNewsletter.

Calrec — committing MXL support to ImPulseV

Calrec publicly committed to adding MXL support to its ImPulseV audio platform — a direct quote from Henry Goodman. Audio joining the MXL domain matters: it's the other half of a real production. Source: Mix.

Matrox & Riedel — fabric and media-function integration

Matrox ORIGIN connected media services over MXL + NMOS BCP-007-03 (with NVIDIA Holoscan for Media); Riedel demonstrated containerized MXL replication and cross-vendor MXL v1.1 interop. Source: TVTechnology · TVNewsCheck.

Reported but not primary-verified

In the interest of being a resource you can trust, these came up across multiple outlets but we have not confirmed the specifics from a primary source — treat as "reported, pending verification":

  • Qvest — reported as hosting "the industry's largest multi-vendor DMF showcase" (Booth 10.C24), said to include Techex, Telestream, Ross Video, Wavelet Beam and Skyline Communications. Headlines corroborate across outlets; the per-vendor contents aren't primary-confirmed here.
  • Lawo — reported MXL / DMF interop work with Grass Valley, and (promotional, unverified) deployment "in international sports productions." We link Lawo as a founding solution provider above but don't restate the sports-deployment claim as fact.

Where we fit

For the record: this site is an independent, one-person open-source lab — not a vendor. We built a full browser-native switcher on MXL + TAMS + NMOS and documented it (the repo, the field findings). We're on this list only as proof that the open stack is reachable without a vendor partnership — which is rather the point of an open standard.
